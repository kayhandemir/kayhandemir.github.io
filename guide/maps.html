Bumper Brawl Guide

Maps

Bumper Brawl has six arenas. Two are open from the start and the other four open as you level up, so each new level-up can bring a new place to fight. Every map has mystery boxes spread around it, health packs that fall from the sky about once a minute, and plenty of things to knock over: street lamps and benches topple, mushrooms squash and gravestones shatter. None of them stop your car, so drive straight through.

Pine Forest

Every player's first map, and the one where the tutorial takes place. A clearing among pine trees and rocks, cut by a shallow river with a couple of bridges. The trees make it hard to see far, which helps when you are being chased and hurts when you are chasing. The river slows you down but does not stop you, so you can cross it anywhere, though the bridges are faster.

Playing it: with trees in the way, a straight shot is hard to line up, so the homing rocket is a strong pick here. Bridges are perfect spots for a mine.

Race Stadium

A race track inside a stadium, with ramps and jumps along the way. It is the most open map in the game: few places to hide and long straight lines, which makes it a good map for the plain rocket and for NOS.

Playing it: keep moving. A car standing still in the open is the easiest target there is. The jumps are a good way to shake off a chaser, who has to line up the same ramp to follow you.

Graveyard

Opens at level 3. A graveyard by night, with gravestones that burst apart when you drive through them and fences that split the ground into lanes. The darkness makes it harder to judge distances, and the narrow paths between graves make for close fights.

Playing it: spikes and blades shine in the narrow lanes, where the others cannot get around you. Use the radar (M) to keep track of cars you cannot see.

Downtown

Opens at level 5. City blocks with streets and crossings, and traffic that keeps driving through the middle of your fight. City cars do not care who is winning: they will cut across a chase or shunt you into someone else.

Playing it: watch the crossings before you cut across them. Around corners a mine or an oil slick catches chasers who cannot see it coming.

Desert Canyon

Opens at level 8. A dusty canyon between tall cliffs, with rocks, cacti, statues and more ramps than any other map. Height matters here: ramps throw you across gaps and over the others, and the cliffs funnel the fight into a few wide paths.

Playing it: TNT thrown into a canyon path hits everyone who comes through it, and a mine at the bottom of a ramp catches whoever lands there.

City Plaza

Opens at level 12, the last map to unlock. A big open square in the city with ramps and jumps all over it. It is fast, open and busy, closer to a skate park than a street, and a good place for long chains of smashes.

Playing it: keep your NOS ready to get back up to speed after a landing, and save God Mode for the moment the pack bunches up in the middle.

Weather

On every map, rain, wind and thunderstorms roll in at random during a match. A wet road loses its grip, so corners take longer and oil slicks become nastier. If you prefer clear skies, or your phone needs the extra frames, you can switch the weather off in the settings.

Each mode plays a little differently on each map. Read the modes guide for the rules.

Bumper Brawl Guide

FAQ

Is Bumper Brawl free?

Yes. The game is free to play in the browser, on this site and on itch.io, and on Android through Google Play. Gold and diamonds can be earned just by playing. The game can show an occasional ad, and the Android version also offers optional in-app purchases.

Which devices does it run on?

Any recent computer browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) and most Android phones from the last few years. In a phone browser, turn the phone sideways; the game is made for landscape. On weaker phones, lower the graphics quality and switch off the weather in the settings for a smoother game.

Do I need an account?

No. You start with a guest profile and a player name and go straight into your first match. Your progress is kept in your profile.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes. Choose Create Game to open a room on our servers, pick the map, mode and match length, and share your invite link or join code. Your friends find the room in the Lobbies list or join with the code. Empty places are filled with bots, which leave as real players join. A room holds six cars.

Is Quick Play online?

Quick Play runs on your own device against bots, so it starts in seconds and works even on a weak connection. For matches against other people, use Create Game or join a room from the Lobbies list.

Where are the servers?

Our game servers are in Europe. Players in Europe, Turkey and the Middle East get the smoothest connection; from further away the game still works, with a little more delay.

The game feels laggy. What can I do?

  • Close other tabs and apps that use the network or the processor.
  • Lower the graphics quality and turn off the weather in the settings.
  • On Wi-Fi, move closer to the router; a mobile connection can be steadier than a weak Wi-Fi signal.
  • If one room is slow, try Quick Play: it does not depend on the connection at all.

My car is stuck on its roof.

Press Space on a keyboard (A on a gamepad) to flip it. If you wait, it also flips back by itself after a few seconds.

How do I unlock the other maps?

By levelling up: Graveyard opens at level 3, Downtown at 5, Desert Canyon at 8 and City Plaza at 12. The maps guide describes each one.

Why doesn't ramming smash anyone?

Because the damage comes from skills. Ramming another car only bumps it; the rockets, mines, TNT, spikes, blades and other weapons from the ? boxes and the market do the damage, and God Mode smashes every car you touch. See the weapons guide.

Are the bots cheating?

No. Bots drive the same cars and use the same weapons from the same mystery boxes as you do. They get better as you level up, from slow rookies at the start to master bots from level 10, so the game keeps up with you.

I found a bug. Where do I report it?

Send an email to studioukgames@gmail.com or use the contact form on the home page. Tell me your device, your browser and what happened just before. A screenshot helps a lot.

How is my data handled?

The privacy policy explains what the game stores, where and for how long, and how to have it deleted.

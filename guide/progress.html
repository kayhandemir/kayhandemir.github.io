Bumper Brawl Guide

Progress and the Garage

Every match you finish moves you forward, win or lose. This page explains how levels, rewards and the garage work, so you know what you are playing for and how to spend what you earn.

Gold and diamonds

Bumper Brawl has two currencies. Gold is the everyday one: you earn it in every match and from quests, chests and level-ups, and you spend it in the garage. Diamonds are rarer, come from level-ups, chests and some rewards, and buy the special items. When you need more gold, you can also turn diamonds into gold.

Smashes also earn coins, which you keep from match to match and spend in the in-match market on weapons and health. (In the Gold Rush mode, the gold lying on the map is the score of that one match.)

Levels

Each match gives you experience, more for smashes, a good finish and a win. Fill the bar and you level up. The main menu always shows what the next level brings, for example gold and diamonds, a new map, a new car or new paints. Levels are also what open the maps: Graveyard at level 3, Downtown at 5, Desert Canyon at 8 and City Plaza at 12.

As you climb, the bots climb with you. At the start they are rookies who react slowly and miss a lot; around level 5 they become normal opponents, and from level 10 you meet master bots that dodge your mines, lead their shots and hold a grudge against whoever hit them last.

Rewards you can collect

  • Match rewards: gold and experience at the end of every match. A good result can come as a reward chest: Gold Pouch, Gold Pile, Gold Hoard or Diamond Chest.
  • Daily quests: a few small goals that change every day, such as "win a match", "smash rivals with rockets" or "pick up health packs". The quests panel shows how far along you are and when new ones arrive.
  • Daily reward: a reward for each day in a row you come back, including new paints and, on day seven, a new car.
  • Night Garage: a chest that starts after you finish a match and opens eight hours later from the menu. Opening it starts the next one.
  • Match of the day: one mode a day pays a bonus the first time you play it.
  • Invites: invite a friend with your link and get gold when they join.

The garage

The garage is where your gold goes. It has three kinds of things to buy:

Cars

There are nine cars: Basher, Bruiser, Raider, Speedster, Cabbie, Enforcer, Inferno, Stinger and Phantom. They differ in speed, acceleration, grip and health, so a heavy car takes more hits while a light one gets away faster. The stats card next to your car in the menu shows the numbers. Some cars unlock with gold, some need a certain level first.

Upgrades

  • Engine (power and top speed), handling (grip and steering) and armor (health) for each car. Upgrades help, but they are capped at a small bonus, so a skilled driver in a stock car can still beat an upgraded one.
  • NOS: bought once per car, after which that car always has its speed boost.
  • Weapon upgrades: more damage, a bigger blast or more mines for the weapons in the mystery boxes.

Looks

Paints and finishes, wheels, spoilers, exhausts, roof toppers, stickers and neon lights. Looks do not change how a car drives, only how it looks to everyone else in the arena.

Your profile

Your level, gold, cars and garage are all kept in your profile. You start with a player name that you can change whenever you like; it is what the others see above your car and on the leaderboards.

Leaderboards

Every match also earns league points for the weekly and all-time leaderboards. The modes guide explains how they are counted.

SmashJeeps

An online vehicle combat arena for 2 to 4 players. Pick a jeep, grab weapons from mystery boxes and smash your friends before the timer runs out.

  • R
    Rockets, mines, spikes, shields and fake boxesEvery weapon has its own tricks, and fake boxes explode on whoever grabs them.
  • +
    Health packs fall from the skyA new pack drops every 40 seconds. Everyone hears it coming.
  • ⇄
    Play with friends onlineHost a match, share the join code, and set the match length from 30 seconds to 10 minutes.
  • ✓
    Your profile follows youStart as a guest, link an account later. Stats, coins and settings are saved.

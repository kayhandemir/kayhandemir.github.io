Bumper Brawl

Free car combat in your browser, on a phone or a computer. Hit Quick Play and you are in a match against bots within seconds: grab weapons from mystery boxes, ram the others off the road and smash more cars than anyone before the clock runs out. Formerly known as SmashJeeps.

  • R
    Nine weapons in the mystery boxesRockets, homing rockets, TNT, mines, spikes, spinning blades, oil slicks, shields, and fake boxes that blow up on whoever grabs them. Fire up NOS to catch anyone, and save God Mode for the right moment: while it lasts, every car you touch is smashed.
  • ⚑
    Four modesSmash the most cars, grab the most gold in Gold Rush, be the last car standing in Last Car Standing, or pass on the ticking bomb in Bomb Tag when you create your own game.
  • ◎
    Six maps that open as you level upStart in Pine Forest and Race Stadium, then open Graveyard by night, Downtown, Desert Canyon and City Plaza. Rain, wind and thunderstorms roll in at random, and a wet road loses its grip.
  • ⇄
    Quick Play or play with friendsQuick Play starts at once on your device, with a short tutorial in your very first match. Create Game opens a room on our servers in Europe; friends join from the lobby list and bots fill the empty places.
  • ✓
    Levels, quests and the garageLevel up, open reward chests, finish daily quests for gold and XP and buy skills mid-match in the market. In the garage, unlock new cars, paint them, add stickers and upgrade them. Your profile follows you.

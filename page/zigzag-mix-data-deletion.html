ZigZag Mix: Data Deletion

Türkçe

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This page explains how to delete your data from ZigZag Mix (Google Play: com.UGame.ZigZagMix), published by UKGames (Uğur Kayhandemir). ZigZag Mix has no account of its own: you play without signing up, and sign-in is only through Google Play Games.

1. Request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to studioukgames@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject "ZigZag Mix data deletion".
  3. Write the Google Play Games gamer name you use in the game, so we can find what belongs to you.

We delete the data we hold that can be linked to you and confirm by email within 30 days. You don't need to give any other personal information.

2. Data you can delete yourself right away

  • Game data on your phone (scores, fruit count, chosen character, settings): uninstall ZigZag Mix, or open Settings › Apps › ZigZag Mix › Storage › Clear data.
  • Google Play Games data (gamer profile for this game, achievements, leaderboard scores, cloud save): open the Google Play Games app, tap your profile, open Settings and choose Delete Play Games account & data. Under Delete individual game data, delete ZigZag Mix (or delete your whole Play Games account). This data is held by Google, and only you can delete it there.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Settings › Google › Ads on your device.

3. What is deleted

  • Your saved progress, achievements and leaderboard scores in Google Play Games (when you delete them as described above).
  • Usage events (Google Analytics for Firebase) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) linked to your installation, when you ask us by email.
  • The game data stored on your phone.

4. What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchases (the "No Ads" purchase): payments are handled by Google Play, and Google keeps purchase records under its own policy for legal and accounting reasons. We never receive your payment details. Your purchase stays valid on your Google account.
  • Advertising data: ads are served by Google AdMob, which keeps ad data under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Crash reports and usage events that are not deleted on request are deleted automatically: crash reports after 90 days, usage events after at most 14 months.

For everything ZigZag Mix processes and why, see our Privacy Policy.

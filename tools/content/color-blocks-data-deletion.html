Last updated: November 7, 2026

This page explains how to delete your data from Color Blocks (Google Play: com.UKGames.ColorBlocks), published by UKGames (Uğur Kayhandemir). Color Blocks has no account of its own: you play without signing up, and sign-in is only through Google Play Games.

1. Request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to studioukgames@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject "Color Blocks data deletion".
  3. Write the Google Play Games gamer name you use in the game, so we can find what belongs to you.

We delete the data we hold that can be linked to you and confirm by email within 30 days. You don't need to give any other personal information.

2. Data you can delete yourself right away

  • Game data on your phone (scores, levels, settings): uninstall Color Blocks, or open Settings › Apps › Color Blocks › Storage › Clear data.
  • Google Play Games data (gamer profile for this game and leaderboard scores): open the Google Play Games app, tap your profile, open Settings and choose Delete Play Games account & data. Under Delete individual game data, delete Color Blocks (or delete your whole Play Games account). This data is held by Google, and only you can delete it there.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Settings › Google › Ads on your device.

3. What is deleted

  • Your Google Play Games profile data and leaderboard scores for Color Blocks (when you delete them as described above).
  • Crash reports and usage events linked to your installation, if they were collected, when you ask us by email.
  • The game data stored on your phone.

4. What is kept, and for how long

  • Advertising data: ads are served by Google AdMob, which keeps ad data under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Crash reports and usage events that are not deleted on request are deleted automatically: crash reports after 90 days, usage events after at most 14 months.

For everything Color Blocks processes and why, see our Privacy Policy.

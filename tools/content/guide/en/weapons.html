In Bumper Brawl the damage comes from skills: ramming another car only bumps it. So every smash comes from the nine weapons hidden in the ? boxes around every map, or from God Mode. You can carry one box weapon at a time: drive through a box, and the weapon it holds shows on your skill button until you use it. Press E on a keyboard or tap the skill button on a phone.

Every weapon also sits in the in-match market (all except the shield), so with enough coins you can line up the one you want instead of waiting for luck. In the garage you can upgrade each weapon for good: more damage, a bigger blast, or more of it.

Rocket

Flies straight ahead and blows up the first car it hits. It is the most direct weapon in the game and rewards aiming: line your nose up with a car that is driving straight toward or away from you, and fire. A rocket that hits a wall still explodes, and the blast hurts anyone standing close to it, so a car hugging a wall is not safe either.

Tip: fire from a little way off rather than point blank. Up close the target can swerve out of your line before you press the button, and you lose the moment.

Homing Rocket

Chases the nearest car on its own, so no aiming is needed. It is the friendliest weapon for new players and the best answer to a fast car that keeps getting away. The catch is "nearest": in a crowd it picks whoever is closest, not the leader you wanted. Pull away from the pack a little first if you want it to go after one car in particular.

TNT

Thrown ahead of you; its big blast hits every car around it. TNT is the crowd weapon. Throw it into the middle of a scrum around a health pack or a mystery box and you can hurt three or four cars at once. Do not throw it at a car right on your bumper: the blast does not care whose TNT it was.

Mine

Dropped behind you; a car that drives over one blows up. Mines are for when you are being chased. Drop them in a narrow spot the chaser has to pass, such as a bridge, a ramp, a gap between buildings or the only way to a health pack. Bots learn to dodge them, so a mine dropped in the open is easy to steer around, while one dropped in a choke point is hard to miss.

Oil Slick

Sprayed behind you; cars chasing you skid and spin out. It does no damage on its own, but a spinning car is a sitting duck for a rocket, and it buys you time to get to a health pack. On a wet map in the rain, when the roads already have less grip, it is even nastier.

Spike

Spikes come out around your car for a while and hurt every car you touch. While they are out you want contact: drive straight at the others, scrape along their sides, push into the pack. Spikes turn a defensive car into a threat, and smashing a rival with spikes is one of the daily quests.

Blades

Spinning blades cut every car that comes close. They work like spikes with a longer reach: you do not have to touch a car, only to be near it. Blades are perfect for parking in the middle of a fight and letting the others come to you, or for chasing a car into a corner.

Shield

A bubble that keeps you from taking damage while it lasts. Pick the moment: when someone is lining up a rocket on you, when the TNT is already in the air, or when you need to cross a crowded area to reach a health pack. The shield only comes from mystery boxes; the market does not sell it.

Fake Box

Looks just like a ? box, but it blows up on whoever grabs it. Drop it where real boxes usually are, or right on the path a chasing car is taking. Players who spot the trick will drive around it, and bots are never sure either. Smashing someone with a fake box is one of the more satisfying daily quests.

Not from the boxes: your shield, NOS and God Mode

Three more tools are not in the boxes at all:

  • Shield (Q): every car carries one. It blocks every hit for five seconds and recharges by itself, so use it whenever you need it.
  • NOS (Shift): a speed boost of about two and a half seconds that comes back every fifteen. You buy it once per car in the garage.
  • God Mode (G): while it lasts, every car you touch is smashed. Each use spends one charge; you earn charges from level-ups and rewards.

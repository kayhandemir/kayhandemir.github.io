Bumper Brawl is easy to pick up: drive, ram, fire. But the players at the top of the scoreboard do a handful of things differently. Here are the habits that make the biggest difference, roughly in the order they help a new player.

1. Always carry a weapon

A car without a weapon can only bump the others, and bumping alone smashes nobody. As soon as you have used your weapon, head for the nearest ? box. Learn where the boxes are on each map, and the arrow at the start of your first match will not be the last time a box saves you. If there is no box nearby, open the market (C) and buy one with your coins.

2. Bump with a purpose

Ramming does no damage on its own; skills do. A bump is still a tool: knock a car off its line so your rocket lands, shove it away from a health pack, or push it back into the TNT you just threw. A bump from the side moves a car further than a head-on one. And when you have spikes, blades or God Mode, every touch counts, so that is the time to drive into the others.

3. Pick on the weak

Look at the health bars above the other cars. A car with a sliver of health left is one rocket from a smash, while a full one will take several. The smash goes to whoever lands the last hit, so finish the weak ones first.

4. Don't be the weak one

When your own health is low, stop fighting and go for a health pack. The game announces each one, and it heals you completely. A shield, an oil slick or a mine behind you will help you get there in one piece.

5. Use your shield and NOS freely

Your shield and NOS come back on their own, so one that is charged and never used is wasted. Use NOS to start every chase and every escape, and the shield (Q) whenever a rocket is coming or you go into a crowd.

6. Keep moving

A car that stands still is an easy target for rockets and TNT. Even while you wait for a box to come back or for a fight to start, drive in circles. Moving targets are much harder to hit.

7. Fight at the edge of a crowd, not in it

In the middle of four cars you take hits from every side. Wait at the edge, pick one target, hit it and get out. If you have TNT, blades or God Mode, that is the moment to go into the middle, and not before.

8. Flip back fast

If a hit or a jump lands you on your roof, press Space (A on a gamepad) right away. The car does flip back by itself after a few seconds, but every second upside down is a second you cannot dodge.

9. Read the mode

Smash rewards hunting. Gold Rush rewards running away once you are ahead. Last Car Standing rewards staying alive and staying inside the zone. Bomb Tag rewards timing. Playing every mode the same way is the most common reason a good driver ends up in the middle of the table. The modes guide has a plan for each one.

10. Spend your gold on one car first

Upgrades and NOS are bought per car. Rather than spreading your gold over several cars, pick one you like driving and make it strong first. NOS is usually the best first purchase: it helps you chase, escape and reach health packs.

Bonus: play your daily quests

Daily quests pay gold for things you would do anyway. Before you start, glance at the quests panel. If today asks for smashes with mines, a few minutes of playing with mines in mind finishes it for you.

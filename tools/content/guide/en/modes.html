Bumper Brawl has four ways to play. Quick Play and the server rooms pick a mode at random for each match, so you will meet most of them without trying. When you open a room with Create Game, you choose the mode yourself. The mode's rules show on screen when a match starts, but here they are in more detail, with a few ideas on how to win each one.

Smash

The rules: smash other cars to score. Whoever has the most smashes when time runs out wins.

Smash is the classic mode and the one you play in your first match. It rewards aggression, but also patience: a car with low health is worth more to you than a full one, because one more hit finishes it. Keep an eye on the health bars above the other cars and go for the weak ones.

  • The smash goes to whoever lands the last hit. If you soften a car up with a weapon, finish it before someone else does.
  • Every second you spend waiting to respawn is a second the others are scoring, so a careful player often beats a reckless one.
  • Chains count. Smash several cars quickly for a double, triple or mega smash, and watch for the bounty on whoever is on a run.

Gold Rush

The rules: gold drops into the arena. Drive into it to collect it. Smash a car and half of its gold spills out for anyone to grab. Whoever carries the most gold when time runs out wins.

Gold Rush turns the usual game around: the leader is a walking treasure chest, and everyone knows it. If you are ahead, stop picking fights and start avoiding them; a mine or an oil slick behind you protects your lead better than a rocket does. If you are behind, hunt the leader. Every smash on them moves a big pile of gold onto the road.

  • Spilled gold scatters. Be the car that arrives right after a smash, not the one that did it.
  • The game shows who has the most gold. Watch that name.

Last Car Standing

The rules: every car has three lives. Get smashed and you lose one; lose them all and you are out. The safe zone shrinks during the match, and outside it your car takes damage. The last car still driving wins.

This is the survival mode. Smashing others matters less than staying alive, and everyone has NOS so nobody gets stuck outside the zone for lack of speed. Early on, collect weapons and let the others wear each other down. As the zone closes, fights become unavoidable. Then a shield, a full health bar and a ready weapon are worth more than anything.

  • Watch the zone warning. "Get into the zone" means your health is already ticking down.
  • Health packs matter more here than in any other mode: one can be a whole extra life.

Bomb Tag

The rules: a ticking bomb rides on one car. If it is on you, ram someone to pass it on. When it blows, whoever passed it last scores 3 points. Smashes score 1. Whoever has the most points wins.

Bomb Tag is the party mode, and only shows up when the host picks it in Create Game (or when it is the weekly mode). Everyone has NOS, so the bomb changes hands fast. With the bomb on you, chase the nearest car and bump it; a touch is enough. Without it, keep your distance from whoever has it, and look for the moment just before it blows to hand it on yourself, because the last pass is the one that scores.

Weekly mode

Every week one mode is the mode of the week, shown on the main menu with a countdown to the next one. It is a good excuse to try a mode you would not pick yourself.

League points

Next to gold and experience, every match you play earns league points for the weekly and all-time leaderboards: 10 for each smash, 30 for a win, and 20, 10 or 5 for finishing first, second or third. Only matches of at least 60 seconds count, so a very short custom match will not pad your total.

Bumper Brawl is a car combat game: you drive a small car around an arena, ram the other cars, pick up weapons and try to smash more cars than anyone else before the clock runs out. This page covers everything you need for your first matches, from the controls to how a match is scored.

Starting a match

From the main menu you have two ways in:

  • Quick Play starts a match against bots straight away, right on your device. Your very first match includes a short tutorial that shows you how to drive, find a mystery box and fire your first weapon. The first few matches are also a little shorter (two and a half minutes) and a little kinder, so you can learn without being swarmed.
  • Create Game opens a room on our own servers in Europe. You pick the map, the mode and the match length (anything from 30 seconds to 10 minutes), and your friends join from the Lobbies list or with your join code. A room has six places; bots fill the ones nobody takes, and step aside when a real player joins.

A normal match lasts four minutes. When the timer reaches zero, the scoreboard shows who won, and everyone earns gold and experience for how they did. The next match starts by itself; you can leave from the results screen or the settings.

Phone and tablet

On a touch screen you choose how you steer in the settings: drag a joystick, tilt the phone, or use arrow buttons. With tilt or arrows you can hold a gas button yourself or switch on auto-accelerate, so your thumb is free for weapons. The skill slot sits at the bottom right, with the NOS button and, just above it, the shield button. Turn the phone sideways: the game is made for landscape.

Gamepad

A controller works too: left stick to drive, Y for your skill, B for the shield, L3 for NOS, A to flip and R3 for the horn.

Smashing cars

Every car has a health bar, and the damage comes from skills: the weapons in the mystery boxes and the market. Ramming another car on its own only bumps it, but a good bump is still useful: it can knock a car off its line, away from a health pack or into the blast of your TNT. When a car's health runs out it is smashed: it bursts apart, the one who did it scores, and the smashed car respawns a few seconds later somewhere else on the map.

Smash several cars quickly and the game calls out a double, triple or mega smash. A player on a long run gets a bounty on their head, and whoever smashes them collects extra coins.

Mystery boxes and weapons

The glowing ? boxes around the map hold a random weapon: rockets, homing rockets, TNT, mines, oil slicks, spikes, spinning blades, a shield, or a fake box you can leave for someone else. Drive through a box to pick it up, then press E (or tap the skill button) to use it. The weapons guide explains each one and when to use it.

Your shield

Besides whatever the boxes give you, every car always carries a shield that blocks every hit for five seconds and then recharges by itself. Press Q (or the button above NOS on a phone) when a rocket is on its way, when you drive into a crowd, or when you need to get through a fight to a health pack.

NOS and God Mode

NOS is a burst of speed of about two and a half seconds that comes back every fifteen. You buy it once for a car in the garage, and from then on that car always has it. Use it to catch someone who is running, to get into range for a weapon, or to escape.

God Mode is rarer: while it lasts, every car you touch is smashed at once. You start with a few charges and earn more from level-ups, chests and rewards, and each use spends one. Save it for when the others are bunched together.

Health packs

About once a minute a health pack falls from the sky, and the game tells everyone it is coming. It fills your health completely, so it is worth racing for when you are low, and worth blocking when a rival is.

The market

Smashes earn coins, and you keep them from match to match. Press C (or tap the market button) to spend them on weapons, so you are not only relying on what the boxes give you. What you buy waits until you load it into your skill slot with the number keys (or a tap). You can own up to three different weapons at once, and the market also sells health for when you are low.
